# Results, Figures & Animations

Catalog of generated plots, diagnostic maps, and animations preserved in the repository.

- **`summary_figures/`** — Summary figures and animations (transmittance, contour plots, Hanle 5-year and monthly PWV plots, MERRA-2 vs. Hanle comparisons, elevation figure, and GIF animations).
- **`era5_merra2/`** — Regional and global ERA5 vs. MERRA-2 correlation plots, cumulative distributions, quartile maps, and brightness temperature comparisons (`new_graphs/` and `map_plots/` contain additional comparison variants and grid map outputs).
- **`animations/`** — Time-evolution MP4 animations of total column water vapor (`tcwv`) and interpolated `tcwv` from ERA5.
